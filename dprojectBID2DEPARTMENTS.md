# Department List

The following departments are available in the Progress Tracking system:

1. **CS Team** - Customer Service Team
2. **PMC** - Production Management Center
3. **Material** - Material Department
4. **Production** - Production Department
5. **Cut and Fold** - Cut and Fold Department
6. **QC** - Quality Control
7. **Shipment** - Shipment Department
8. **Account** - Accounting Department

## Department Colors (for badges)

- CS Team: Blue (#2196F3)
- PMC: Purple (#9C27B0)
- Material: Orange (#FF9800)
- Production: Green (#4CAF50)
- Cut and Fold: Teal (#009688)
- QC: Red (#F44336)
- Shipment: Brown (#795548)
- Account: Blue Grey (#607D8B)

## Usage

These departments are used in:
- Progress Tracking view for scanning PO progress
- Order Status view to show the latest progress status for each PO
